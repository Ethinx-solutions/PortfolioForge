## 🎯 CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

**Campaign Name:** Find T-Dog  
**Objective:** Drive engagement, comments, viral growth, and community building  
**Duration:** Ongoing (weekly carousel rotation)  
**Primary Channel:** Instagram (carousel posts)  
**Secondary Channels:** TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter/X  
**Target Audience:** Aspiring founders, digital entrepreneurs, ETHINX community  

---

## 🎮 GAMIFICATION MECHANICS

### **How It Works**

1. **Post Carousel** — Each carousel has T-Dog hidden in a different location
2. **Challenge** — "Can you find T-Dog? 👀 Type 'T-Dog' in the comments if you spot him!"
3. **Engagement** — Users scroll through all 5 slides looking for T-Dog
4. **Comment** — Users comment "T-Dog" when they find him
5. **Reward** — You send them something cool (mystery reward)

### **Why This Works**

**Algorithmic Benefits:**
- Increased time-on-post (users scroll all 5 slides)
- Increased comments (engagement metric)
- Increased shares (users tag friends: "Did you find T-Dog?")
- Carousel format naturally drives engagement

**Psychological Benefits:**
- **Gamification** — People love games and challenges
- **FOMO** — "What am I missing? Let me look closer"
- **Community** — Inside joke creates belonging
- **Reward Loop** — Mystery prize creates dopamine hit
- **Repeat Engagement** — Every new carousel is a new hunt

**Business Benefits:**
- Builds email list (collect emails for rewards)
- Creates brand awareness
- Drives traffic to landing page
- Builds community loyalty
- Generates user-generated content

## 🎬 CONTENT VARIATIONS

### **Variation 1: "Spot the Difference"**

Instead of finding T-Dog, find what's different between two carousel slides.

**Example:** "Slide 1 vs Slide 2 - what changed? Comment your answer!"

### **Variation 2: "T-Dog Trivia"**

Hide T-Dog + ask a trivia question about ETHINX.

**Example:** "Find T-Dog AND answer: How many revenue modes does PromptForge have?"

### **Variation 3: "T-Dog Says"**

Each carousel has T-Dog with a different quote. Find him and guess what he's saying.

**Example:** "What's T-Dog saying? Find him and guess in the comments!"

### **Variation 4: "T-Dog's Location"**

Hide T-Dog in increasingly difficult spots as the month progresses.

**Week 1:** Easy (obvious location)  
**Week 2:** Medium (requires looking)  
**Week 3:** Hard (well-hidden)  
**Week 4:** Expert (very subtle)
