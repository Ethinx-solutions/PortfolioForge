**Format:** Instagram Carousel (5 slides per post)

## 🎨 CAROUSEL DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### **Visual Consistency**
- Navy Blue (#0F3A7D) background on all slides
- Coral Orange (#FF6B35) accents for CTAs and highlights
- White text for maximum contrast
- Consistent typography (Montserrat Bold headlines, Inter body)

### **Information Hierarchy**
- Slide 1: Aspiration (hook them)
- Slide 2: Proof (show it works)
- Slide 3: System (explain how)
- Slide 4: Proof of Concept (demonstrate live)
- Slide 5: Action (close the loop)

### **Micro-Interactions**
- Each slide builds on previous (narrative arc)
- Visual progression from concept to action
- Emotional journey: curiosity → belief → action
