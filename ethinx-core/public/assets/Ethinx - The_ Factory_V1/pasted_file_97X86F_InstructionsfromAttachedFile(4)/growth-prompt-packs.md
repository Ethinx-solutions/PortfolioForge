## OVERVIEW

The Growth Prompt Pack System provides 6 specialized packs designed for acquiring users, driving traffic, and scaling growth. Each pack includes complete prompts tailored to specific growth channels and strategies.

**System Features:**
- **6 Specialized Growth Packs** covering major growth channels
- **27 Detailed Prompts** with growth-specific context variables
- **Integrated Visualisation Hooks** for growth dashboards and diagrams
- **Channel-Specific Strategies** for each growth method
- **Measurement and Analytics Frameworks** for tracking growth
- **Quality Assurance Framework** with mandatory checks
