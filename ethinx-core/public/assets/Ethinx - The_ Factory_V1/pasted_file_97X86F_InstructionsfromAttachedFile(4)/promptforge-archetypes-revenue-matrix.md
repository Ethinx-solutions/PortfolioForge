## Part 1: Asset Archetype Deep Dives

### 1. Micro-SaaS

**Definition**: Lightweight software-as-a-service targeting a specific niche or pain point. Typically built by solo founders or small teams, focused on solving one problem exceptionally well.

**Core Characteristics**:
- Solves a specific, well-defined problem
- Targets a niche audience (not mass market)
- Recurring revenue model (ideally)
- Low infrastructure costs
- Minimal ongoing maintenance (if well-built)

**Natural Revenue Models**: Subscription, Freemium → Upsell, Affiliate

**Typical Passive Score**: 6–8 (recurring revenue, but requires ongoing customer support and feature development)

**Examples of Micro-SaaS**:
- **Notion API Client**: Allows non-technical users to automate Notion workflows
- **SEO Tool for Niche Keywords**: Identifies low-competition, high-volume keywords for specific niches
- **Email Warm-Up Tool**: Automatically warms up email accounts for cold outreach
- **Social Media Analytics Dashboard**: Aggregates analytics from multiple platforms
- **Scheduling Tool for Service Providers**: Manages appointments and sends reminders

---

### 2. Automation Engine

**Definition**: A tool or workflow that automates a repetitive business process for users. Can be a standalone product or integrated into existing platforms.

**Core Characteristics**:
- Solves a repetitive, time-consuming process
- Highly automatable (minimal human intervention)
- Can be deployed across multiple platforms (Zapier, Make, custom integrations)
- Scales without proportional increase in effort
- Often targets B2B audiences

**Natural Revenue Models**: Subscription, Affiliate, Passive Income

**Typical Passive Score**: 8–9 (highly automatable, low maintenance once deployed)

**Examples of Automation Engines**:
- **LinkedIn Outreach Automation**: Sends personalized LinkedIn messages and tracks responses
- **Invoice Processing Automation**: Extracts data from invoices and updates accounting software
- **Social Media Scheduling Automation**: Schedules posts across multiple platforms
- **Email Lead Nurturing Automation**: Sends personalized email sequences based on behavior
- **Customer Support Ticket Automation**: Routes tickets and assigns them to agents

---

### 3. Template/Product Pack

**Definition**: A pre-built, reusable template or pack (Notion, Figma, Airtable, Canva, etc.) sold as a digital product.

**Core Characteristics**:
- Zero marginal cost to deliver (digital product)
- One-time creation effort, infinite sales potential
- Highly scalable (no inventory, no shipping)
- Can be sold on multiple marketplaces
- Passive delivery (auto-delivery via Zapier)

**Natural Revenue Models**: One-Time Product, Passive Income, Affiliate

**Typical Passive Score**: 9–10 (minimal maintenance, fully automatable delivery and updates)

**Examples of Template/Product Packs**:
- **Notion Project Management Template**: Comprehensive system for managing projects and tasks
- **Figma Design System**: Complete design system with components, colors, typography
- **Airtable CRM Template**: Full CRM system for managing contacts and deals
- **Canva Social Media Template Pack**: 100+ templates for social media posts
- **Google Sheets Budget Tracker**: Comprehensive budget tracking and expense management
